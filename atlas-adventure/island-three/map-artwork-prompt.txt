GOAL
Create a rich, coherent volcanic archive map and its separate interactive object assets. The result must look like one believable location, not ten unrelated icons. Match the premium painterly-isometric adventure style of Cloud Harbor and Gale Garden.

IMPORTANT
- Create original, copyright-safe artwork.
- Do not draw the explorer, Spark, interface controls, labels, numbers, buttons, or written words.
- The explorer and Spark are existing separate assets.
- Every object must have the same camera angle, scale, lighting, outline treatment, and colour language.
- Use a three-quarter elevated camera with readable silhouettes and a 16:9 composition.
- Keep the lower and middle parts of the scene playable and avoid placing important art behind the top-centre movement controls.
